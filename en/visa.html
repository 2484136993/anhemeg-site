📋 Visa Types for Medical Tourism to China

Most international patients who travel to Shanghai for cosmetic surgery use one of the following visa types. Whether you are considering foreigner plastic surgery in Shanghai or exploring medical tourism Shanghai plastic surgery options, securing the right visa is your first step.

Tourist Visa (L Visa)

The most common and recommended option for medical tourists. The L Visa allows you to travel to China for tourism purposes, which includes visiting for cosmetic surgery consultations and procedures. No medical documentation is required for the application.

  • Single entry: valid for 30-90 days
  • Double entry: valid for 180 days
  • Multiple entry: valid for 1-10 years (varies by nationality)

Business Visa (M Visa)

If you are combining your medical trip with business activities in China, the M Visa may be appropriate. Requires an invitation letter from a Chinese business entity.

Medical Visa (S2 Visa)

For patients needing extended stays for medical treatment. Requires medical documentation and a letter from the treating facility. Processing may take longer than a tourist visa.

Important: Visa policies vary by nationality and may change. Always check with your local Chinese embassy or consulate for the most current requirements before applying.

📄 Required Documents

Standard documents required for a China tourist visa (L Visa) application:

  1. Valid Passport — At least 6 months validity remaining with blank visa pages
  2. Visa Application Form — Completed online via the Chinese Visa Application Service Center website
  3. Recent Photo — 2-inch (48mm × 33mm) color photo with white background, taken within the last 6 months
  4. Flight Itinerary — Round-trip flight booking confirmation
  5. Hotel Reservation — Accommodation booking for your stay in Shanghai
  6. Financial Proof — Bank statements showing sufficient funds for your trip
  7. Travel Insurance — Recommended for medical tourists (Anhe Meige can advise on suitable plans)

Additional documents may be required depending on your nationality. Some countries qualify for visa-free entry or visa-on-arrival — check current regulations for your country.

📝 Application Process

1

Prepare Documents

Gather all required documents. Anhe Meige can provide assistance with hotel booking confirmation and a letter of introduction if needed.

2

Submit Application Online

Complete the visa application form on the Chinese Visa Application Service Center website and schedule an appointment.

3

Visit Visa Center

Attend your appointment at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. Submit documents and provide biometrics (fingerprints) if required.

4

Wait for Processing

Standard processing takes 4-5 business days. Expedited service (2-3 business days) may be available for an additional fee.

5

Collect Your Visa

Pick up your passport with the visa stamp, or arrange for courier delivery if available in your area.
