Your Questions About Plastic Surgery in Shanghai, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start the plastic surgery process in Shanghai? +

You can contact us via WhatsApp or WeChat for a free consultation. Our team at Anhe Meige will guide you through every step — from selecting procedures, applying for a visa, booking flights, to airport pickup and full medical interpretation. Plastic surgery in Shanghai has never been easier with our professional support.

Do I need to speak Chinese? +

No! Anhe Meige provides a Vietnamese/English-speaking medical interpreter who accompanies you throughout your entire journey — from airport pickup to departure. All communication with doctors is accurately translated, so you can focus on your care.

Is it difficult to get a Chinese tourist visa? +

A Chinese tourist visa (L visa) is not difficult to obtain if you prepare the required documents. We provide detailed guidance, itinerary letters, and hotel confirmations to increase your approval rate. Processing usually takes 5-7 business days.

How much does plastic surgery in Shanghai cost? +

Costs depend on the procedures you choose. Total expenses (surgery + flights + accommodation) are typically 30-50% less than in South Korea for comparable quality. For example: double eyelid surgery from $400-800, rhinoplasty from $800-1,500. Contact us for a detailed quote.

How long do I need to stay in Shanghai? +

Usually 5-7 days is sufficient for most procedures. Double eyelid surgery or filler injections may only need 3-5 days. V-line jaw contouring patients should stay 7-10 days for proper monitoring. We will advise the optimal stay duration for your specific case.

Who monitors my recovery after I return home? +

Anhe Meige provides free remote follow-up care. Doctors will conduct video check-ups, and you can send photos via WeChat/WhatsApp for progress monitoring. All questions are answered promptly throughout your recovery period.

Is Anhe Meige a reputable clinic? +

Anhe Meige is a reputable cosmetic surgery clinic in Shanghai with experienced plastic surgeons and modern facilities. We strictly comply with medical regulations and prioritize patient safety — a trusted destination for international patients seeking cosmetic surgery in Shanghai.

Is it dangerous to have cosmetic surgery in China? +

All surgeries carry some risk. At Anhe Meige, we minimize risks through: modern equipment, experienced surgeons, thorough pre-operative consultation, and careful post-operative monitoring. You will receive detailed information about risks and benefits before making any decision.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept cash (CNY/USD), bank transfers, WeChat Pay, Alipay, and international credit cards. Our consultation team will guide you to the most convenient payment method for your situation.

Do you help with flight and hotel bookings? +

Yes! We provide comprehensive support: advice on affordable flights, hotel bookings near the clinic, free airport pickup, and a personal interpreter throughout your stay in Shanghai. You can focus entirely on your recovery.

When is the best time to have plastic surgery in Shanghai? +

You can visit any time of year. However, you may want to avoid summer (June-August) if you're sensitive to hot, humid weather. Autumn (September-November) is the most ideal time with cool, comfortable weather that's favorable for recovery.

Is there insurance coverage for cosmetic surgery? +

Cosmetic surgery is typically not covered by medical insurance as it's considered an elective procedure. However, we provide warranty coverage for surgical procedures, ensuring results meet our commitments. Please consult with us for detailed warranty terms.

Can I have multiple procedures done at the same time? +

Yes, depending on your health condition and the scope of the procedures. Many patients choose to combine double eyelid surgery with rhinoplasty in a single visit. Our surgeons will evaluate and recommend the safest approach for you.
