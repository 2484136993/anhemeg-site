🏥 General Post-Surgery Care Instructions

Following your cosmetic procedure at Anhe Meige, proper aftercare is essential for optimal recovery. Here are general guidelines that apply to most procedures:

  • Rest: Get plenty of rest for the first 48-72 hours. Avoid strenuous activities.
  • Elevation: Keep your head elevated (use 2-3 pillows) to reduce swelling, especially for facial procedures.
  • Medication: Take prescribed medications as directed. Do not skip doses or self-adjust.
  • Cold Compress: Apply cold compresses as directed (typically 15 minutes on, 15 minutes off) for the first 48 hours.
  • Hydration: Drink plenty of water and maintain a healthy diet rich in protein and vitamins.
  • Avoid: No alcohol, smoking, or blood-thinning medications (aspirin, ibuprofen) unless approved by your surgeon.
  • Sun Protection: Avoid direct sunlight on treated areas. Use SPF 30+ sunscreen when going outdoors.
  • Follow-up: Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments, including remote video consultations after you return home.

📊 Recovery Timeline by Procedure

👁️ Double Eyelid Surgery

Timeframe What to Expect
Day 1-3 Swelling and bruising around the eyes. Cold compresses recommended.
Day 5-7 Stitches removed. Swelling significantly reduced.
Day 7-10 Most patients can return to daily activities. Light makeup may be worn.
1-3 months Final results as residual swelling fully resolves.

👃 Rhinoplasty

Timeframe What to Expect
Day 1-3 Nasal splint in place. Facial swelling and congestion common.
Day 7 Splint and external stitches removed. Noticeable improvement in swelling.
Day 10-14 Most bruising resolved. Can return to work and social activities.
3-6 months Final shape as all swelling subsides. Nose continues to refine.

💎 V-line Jaw Contouring

Timeframe What to Expect
Day 1-3 Significant facial swelling. Liquid diet required. Bandage in place.
Day 7-10 Swelling begins to decrease. Transition to soft foods.
Day 14-21 Most patients resume normal activities. Some residual swelling.
3-6 months Final contour visible as all swelling resolves.

🌸 Breast Augmentation

Timeframe What to Expect
Day 1-3 Chest tightness and soreness. Surgical bra must be worn. Limited arm movement.
Day 7-10 Stitches removed. Can resume light daily activities.
4-6 weeks Full recovery. Can resume exercise and normal activities.
3-6 months Implants settle into final position. Scars begin to fade.

🚨 Emergency Contact

If you experience any of the following after your procedure, please contact us immediately:

  • Excessive bleeding that does not stop with gentle pressure
  • Sudden increase in pain not relieved by prescribed medication
  • Fever above 38.5°C (101.3°F)
  • Signs of infection (redness, warmth, pus at the incision site)
  • Difficulty breathing or swallowing
  • Any sudden or unusual changes in the treated area

💡 Recovery Tips

🥗

Nutrition

Eat a balanced diet rich in protein, vitamin C, and zinc. Avoid salty foods that may increase swelling. Stay well-hydrated.

😴

Sleep

Get 8-10 hours of sleep per night. Sleep on your back with your head elevated for at least 2 weeks after facial procedures.

🚿

Hygiene

Follow your surgeon's instructions for wound care. Avoid submerging incision sites in water until cleared by your doctor.

🏃

Activity

Gradually increase activity as directed. Avoid vigorous exercise for 4-6 weeks. Short walks are encouraged to promote circulation.

😊

Mental Well-being

It's normal to feel emotional after surgery. The "post-surgery blues" are common. Stay connected with our support team.

📸

Photo Documentation

Take daily photos of your recovery progress to share during follow-up consultations. This helps our team track your healing.
