Your Complete Plastic Surgery Journey in Shanghai

Understanding what to expect at every stage of your plastic surgery journey in Shanghai helps you prepare mentally and logistically, leading to a smoother and more positive experience. This guide walks you through the typical timeline from initial consultation to final results, based on the experiences of international patients at Anhe Meige. While individual experiences vary based on the specific procedure and personal factors, this overview provides a reliable framework for planning your medical tourism trip.

Before You Travel: Remote Consultation and Planning

The process typically begins 2–4 weeks before your travel date with a remote consultation. During this video call, you will discuss your aesthetic goals with an Anhe Meige surgeon (with English interpretation). The surgeon will ask about your medical history, previous procedures, and specific concerns. You may be asked to send photos from multiple angles for preliminary assessment. Based on this consultation, the surgeon will provide initial recommendations, a cost estimate, and pre-operative instructions. You will also receive guidance on visa application, travel planning, and what to pack. This pre-travel preparation ensures that both you and the medical team are well-prepared when you arrive in Shanghai.

Arriving in Shanghai: Day 1–2

Upon arrival at Shanghai Pudong or Hongqiao Airport, Anhe Meige's pickup service will transport you to your hotel. Most patients spend their first day resting and adjusting to the time zone. On day two, you will visit the clinic for your in-person consultation. This is more detailed than the remote consultation and includes a physical examination, facial measurements, and in-depth discussion of the surgical plan. The surgeon will explain the procedure, expected outcomes, risks, and recovery timeline. You will have the opportunity to ask questions and view before-and-after photos. If you are comfortable with the plan, you will sign the treatment agreement and schedule the procedure.

Procedure Day: What Happens

On the day of your procedure at Anhe Meige, you will arrive at the clinic at the scheduled time. Pre-operative preparations include vital sign checks, marking of surgical sites, and final consultation with the anesthesiologist if general anesthesia is planned. Your English interpreter will be present throughout to translate and provide reassurance. The procedure duration varies by type — double eyelid surgery takes approximately 1–2 hours, rhinoplasty 2–3 hours, and jaw contouring 3–4 hours. After the procedure, you will spend time in the recovery room under nursing supervision before being cleared to return to your hotel.

Recovery in Shanghai: Days 1–14

The recovery period in Shanghai is a crucial part of your experience. For most facial procedures, the first 2–3 days involve the most swelling and discomfort, managed with prescribed medications. By day 5–7, initial swelling subsides significantly and most patients feel comfortable moving around. Follow-up visits at Anhe Meige are typically scheduled at day 3, day 7, and day 10–14, depending on the procedure. During these visits, the surgical team checks your healing progress, removes sutures if necessary, and adjusts your care plan. Having these follow-ups in person, with English communication support, provides reassurance during the most vulnerable phase of recovery.

Returning Home and Long-Term Recovery

Before you leave Shanghai, Anhe Meige provides comprehensive post-operative instructions in English, including wound care guidelines, activity restrictions, dietary recommendations, and signs to watch for that may require medical attention. You will also receive a schedule for remote follow-up consultations via video call. These typically occur at 1 month, 3 months, and 6 months post-procedure. Full results from most cosmetic procedures become apparent over 6–12 months as residual swelling resolves and tissues settle into their final position. Anhe Meige remains available for questions and support throughout this entire period, ensuring you never feel abandoned after returning home.
