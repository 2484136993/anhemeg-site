Initial Contact Process

International patients can initiate contact with Anhe Meige through WeChat, phone, or email. Providing basic information helps us prepare for your consultation visit.

Upon arrival, our team conducts a structured assessment to understand your needs and concerns. This includes reviewing your goals and discussing available options.

Professional Recommendations

Based on the assessment, our consultants provide professional recommendations tailored to your individual situation. All suggestions are explained in detail.

After the consultation, we provide comprehensive documentation and schedule follow-up appointments as needed. We remain available for any questions you may have.
