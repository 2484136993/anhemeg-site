Rhinoplasty Pricing Breakdown for 2026

Types of Rhinoplasty Procedures and Their Costs

The total cost of your rhinoplasty in Shanghai will depend significantly on the specific procedure type required to achieve your aesthetic goals. Understanding the different procedure categories helps you anticipate costs and make informed decisions.

Augmentation Rhinoplasty

Augmentation rhinoplasty focuses on increasing nasal bridge height and improving overall nasal definition. This procedure is particularly popular among patients with flatter nasal bridges seeking more pronounced facial contours.

  • Silicone implant rhinoplasty: ¥15,000 – ¥25,000 (approximately $2,100–$3,500 USD)
  • ePTFE/Gore-Tex implant rhinoplasty: ¥20,000 – ¥35,000 (approximately $2,800–$4,900 USD)
  • Autologous cartilage (ear cartilage) augmentation: ¥25,000 – ¥40,000 (approximately $3,500–$5,600 USD)

Augmentation rhinoplasty with implants offers immediate results with relatively shorter surgery times and recovery periods. The choice between implant materials affects both cost and longevity, with autologous cartilage generally providing more natural integration but requiring additional harvesting procedures.

Structured Rhinoplasty

Structured rhinoplasty represents a more comprehensive approach to nasal refinement, addressing multiple aesthetic and functional concerns simultaneously. This technique is particularly suitable for patients requiring significant nasal transformation or those seeking more durable, natural-looking results.

  • Ear cartilage structured rhinoplasty: ¥30,000 – ¥45,000 (approximately $4,200–$6,300 USD)
  • Rib cartilage structured rhinoplasty: ¥40,000 – ¥60,000 (approximately $5,600–$8,400 USD)
  • Septal cartilage structured rhinoplasty: ¥35,000 – ¥55,000 (approximately $4,900–$7,700 USD)

Structured rhinoplasty techniques provide superior support and contouring capabilities, particularly for patients with weaker nasal foundations or those requiring extensive reconstruction. The use of autologous cartilage reduces risks of rejection and provides more natural-feeling results.

Revision Rhinoplasty

Revision rhinoplasty addresses complications or unsatisfactory results from previous nose surgeries. These procedures are inherently more complex due to the presence of scar tissue and altered anatomy, requiring exceptional surgical skill and experience.

  • Minor revision rhinoplasty: ¥35,000 – ¥55,000 (approximately $4,900–$7,700 USD)
  • Moderate revision rhinoplasty: ¥50,000 – ¥80,000 (approximately $7,000–$11,200 USD)
  • Major/complex revision rhinoplasty: ¥80,000 – ¥120,000+ (approximately $11,200–$16,800+ USD)

Revision cases require careful evaluation and planning. Patients considering revision surgery should seek surgeons with specific experience in corrective procedures, as these operations demand advanced technical capabilities.

Tip Surgery and Refinement

Some patients require only tip refinement without significant changes to the nasal bridge. Tip plasty or tip rhinoplasty focuses specifically on reshaping and repositioning the nasal tip for better definition and projection.

  • Isolated tip rhinoplasty: ¥15,000 – ¥30,000 (approximately $2,100–$4,200 USD)
  • Tip rhinoplasty with cartilage grafting: ¥25,000 – ¥40,000 (approximately $3,500–$5,600 USD)

Tip surgery offers a more limited scope of improvement, making it suitable for patients whose primary concern is nasal tip appearance rather than overall nasal structure.

Factors Influencing Rhinoplasty Costs

Surgeon Experience and Expertise

The surgeon's qualifications and experience significantly impact rhinoplasty pricing. Highly experienced surgeons who specialize in nasal surgery typically command higher fees, reflecting their accumulated expertise and track record of successful outcomes.

Surgeons with 20+ years of experience performing rhinoplasty procedures have developed refined techniques and extensive case exposure. While their fees may be higher, the reduced risk of complications and higher satisfaction rates often make this investment worthwhile. Anhe Meige and similar established clinics typically feature surgeons at various experience levels, allowing patients to choose according to their preferences and budgets.

Facility Standards and Accreditation

The surgical facility's classification and accreditation affect both safety standards and procedural costs. Higher-tier facilities with state-of-the-art equipment, board-certified anesthesiologists, and comprehensive emergency protocols naturally incur higher operational costs.

Patients should verify that their chosen facility maintains appropriate medical licenses and accreditation. Shanghai's leading medical tourism facilities typically meet or exceed international standards for patient safety and care quality.

Anesthesia Considerations

General anesthesia provides the most comfortable surgical experience for complex rhinoplasty procedures, while local anesthesia with sedation may be appropriate for simpler cases. The type of anesthesia chosen affects both cost and recovery experience.

  • General anesthesia: ¥3,000 – ¥6,000 (including anesthesiologist fees)
  • Local anesthesia with sedation: ¥1,500 – ¥3,000

Professional anesthesia administration significantly impacts surgical precision and patient comfort during rhinoplasty procedures.

Geographic Location Within Shanghai

Facility location within Shanghai can influence pricing due to variations in real estate and operational costs. Central business district locations typically have higher overhead expenses that may be reflected in procedure pricing. However, many reputable clinics are strategically located with convenient transportation access, making them accessible regardless of specific district.

Additional Costs Beyond the Procedure

Pre-Operative Consultations and Assessments

Most clinics include initial consultations within their overall pricing structure. However, some complex cases may require additional imaging studies or specialist assessments that incur separate charges.

Typical pre-operative expenses include:

  • Blood tests and medical clearance: ¥300 – ¥800
  • Nasal imaging/scan: ¥500 – ¥1,500 (if required)
  • 3D facial analysis: ¥800 – ¥2,000 (at select facilities)

Post-Operative Care and Medications

Recovery-related expenses should be factored into your overall budget. Quality post-operative care contributes significantly to successful outcomes.

  • Prescription medications (antibiotics, pain management, anti-swelling): ¥300 – ¥800
  • Nasal splint and cast: Usually included in surgical package
  • Follow-up consultations: Typically included for specified period
  • Silicone nose strips/support: ¥100 – ¥300

Accommodation and Living Expenses

International patients should budget for accommodation and daily expenses during their Shanghai stay. Most rhinoplasty patients require approximately 7-14 days in Shanghai for initial recovery and follow-up care.

  • Hotel accommodation (mid-range): ¥300 – ¥800 per night
  • Serviced apartments (for extended stays): ¥500 – ¥1,200 per night
  • Daily meals and transportation: ¥150 – ¥400 per day

Anhe Meige can recommend nearby hotels and serviced apartments that cater specifically to medical tourism patients, often offering discounted rates for extended stays.

Interpretation Services

For non-Chinese speaking patients, interpretation services are essential for effective communication with medical staff. Many Shanghai clinics catering to international patients provide interpretation as part of their service package.

  • Included interpretation: Offered by medical tourism clinics like Anhe Meige
  • Private medical interpreter (if needed): ¥500 – ¥1,500 per day

Budget Planning Tips for International Patients

1. Request Comprehensive Quotes

When evaluating rhinoplasty costs in Shanghai, request itemized quotes that clearly outline all expected expenses. Reputable clinics like Anhe Meige provide transparent pricing breakdowns that help you understand exactly what is included.

A comprehensive quote should specify:

  • Surgeon's fees
  • Facility/operating room charges
  • Anesthesia fees
  • Implant or graft material costs
  • Post-operative medications and supplies
  • Follow-up consultation fees
  • Interpreter services (if applicable)

2. Consider Total Journey Costs

Effective budgeting requires considering the complete medical journey, not just the surgical procedure. Factor in flights, accommodation, meals, transportation, and any required travel insurance. Many patients find that even with these additional expenses, the total cost remains significantly lower than surgery in their home country.

3. Plan for Contingencies

While complications from rhinoplasty are relatively uncommon with qualified surgeons, it is prudent to budget for potential unexpected expenses. Consider setting aside an additional 10-15% of your procedure budget for contingencies such as extended accommodation if recovery takes longer than expected or additional follow-up visits.

4. Avoid Compromising Quality for Price

The lowest price does not always represent the best value. Extremely discounted rhinoplasty may indicate compromises in surgeon experience, facility standards, or material quality. Focus on finding the best balance of quality and affordability rather than simply choosing the cheapest option.

Payment Policies and Methods

Accepted Payment Methods

Most Shanghai medical facilities accept international payment methods:

  • Wire transfer: Often available for larger deposits
  • Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, and occasionally Amex accepted (may incur processing fees)
  • Alipay/WeChat Pay: Widely accepted for CNY transactions
  • Cash: RMB accepted (some clinics offer discounts for cash payment)

Deposit Requirements

Most clinics require a deposit to secure your surgical date. This deposit typically ranges from ¥5,000 – ¥20,000 and is applied toward your total procedure cost.

Currency Considerations

International patients should verify current exchange rates and factor in potential currency fluctuation when budgeting. Some clinics offer price locking in USD for a specified period, providing budget certainty for international patients.
