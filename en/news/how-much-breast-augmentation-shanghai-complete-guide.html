Breast Augmentation Procedure Types and Their Costs in Shanghai

Breast augmentation encompasses several different techniques and approaches, each with distinct pricing structures. Understanding these options is essential for accurate budgeting and realistic expectations.

Silicone Breast Implants

Silicone implants remain the most popular choice worldwide and are widely available in Shanghai. These implants provide a natural feel and appearance, with various profiles and sizes to suit different body types and aesthetic goals.

Estimated Cost in Shanghai: $4,500 – $9,500 USD

This price range typically includes high-quality silicone implants from reputable manufacturers, surgeon fees, anesthesia, facility charges, and initial follow-up consultations. The variation in pricing depends on implant brand (Motiva, Mentor, or other FDA-approved options), implant size, and the complexity of your specific case.

Saline Breast Implants

Saline implants offer an alternative for patients who prefer implants filled with sterile saltwater solution. While less commonly chosen than silicone, they remain a viable option with their own advantages.

Estimated Cost in Shanghai: $3,500 – $7,000 USD

Saline implants are generally less expensive than silicone options. One advantage is that the implant is filled during surgery, allowing for smaller incisions. However, many patients and surgeons prefer silicone for its more natural feel.

Fat Transfer Breast Augmentation

For patients seeking a more natural approach, fat transfer breast augmentation uses the patient's own fat harvested from other areas through liposuction. This procedure offers dual benefits: body contouring where fat is removed and subtle breast enhancement.

Estimated Cost in Shanghai: $5,000 – $12,000 USD

The higher cost reflects the complexity of combining liposuction with breast augmentation. Patients typically need adequate fat reserves for harvesting, and the procedure requires specialized surgical expertise. Results are generally more subtle than implant-based augmentation, with increases typically limited to one to two cup sizes.

Combined Procedures (Breast Augmentation with Lift)

Many patients choosing breast augmentation also benefit from a concurrent breast lift (mastopexy). This combination addresses both volume enhancement and tissue repositioning for a more comprehensive rejuvenation.

Estimated Cost in Shanghai: $7,000 – $15,000 USD

The combined procedure addresses sagging tissue while adding volume, resulting in a more youthful appearance. This option is particularly popular among patients who have experienced pregnancy, breastfeeding, or significant weight changes.

Budgeting Tips for Your Breast Augmentation in Shanghai

Effective budgeting extends beyond the quoted procedure price. Consider these factors when planning your medical tourism journey to Shanghai.

Create a Comprehensive Budget

Start with the procedure cost as your foundation, then add: round-trip airfare for you and any companion, accommodation for the recommended stay (typically 7-14 days for initial recovery and follow-up), ground transportation in Shanghai, meals and daily expenses, travel insurance with medical coverage, and contingency fund for unexpected expenses (recommend 15-20% buffer).

Plan for Recovery Time

Breast augmentation typically requires 7-10 days in Shanghai before air travel is safe. During this time, you'll need comfortable accommodation, easy access to your medical facility for follow-up appointments, and potentially assistance with daily activities during the initial recovery phase. Factor these needs into your accommodation selection and companion planning.

Consider Timing Strategically

Some facilities offer seasonal promotions or package deals for international patients. Booking during off-peak seasons may provide cost benefits. However, prioritize surgeon expertise and facility quality over minor price variations—your results and safety matter far more than small savings.

Frequently Asked Questions About Breast Augmentation Costs in Shanghai

Is breast augmentation in Shanghai safe?

Yes, when performed at accredited facilities by qualified surgeons, breast augmentation in Shanghai meets or exceeds international safety standards. Anhe Meige maintains JCI-equivalent accreditation and employs surgeons with extensive experience in aesthetic procedures.

How long should I stay in Shanghai after breast augmentation?

We recommend staying in Shanghai for at least 7-10 days following your procedure. This allows for initial recovery, removal of any drains or sutures, and a follow-up evaluation before air travel. Your surgeon will provide specific guidance based on your individual recovery progress.

What is included in the quoted price for breast augmentation?

Standard inclusions typically encompass surgeon fees, anesthesia, facility charges, implant costs (when applicable), basic medications, compression garments, and follow-up consultations. Request a detailed breakdown when obtaining quotes to understand exactly what's included.

Will I need revision surgery, and is it included in the original cost?

Complication rates for breast augmentation are generally low when performed by experienced surgeons. However, some patients ultimately desire size adjustments or experience capsular contracture requiring treatment. Revision surgery is typically charged separately from the original procedure unless explicitly included in a warranty package.

Can I combine breast augmentation with other procedures?

Yes, many patients choose combination procedures such as breast augmentation with lift, liposuction, or other body contouring treatments. Combining procedures can be cost-effective and reduces overall recovery time compared to separate surgeries. Your surgeon can advise on safe and appropriate combinations based on your health and goals.

How do I choose between silicone and saline implants?

Your surgeon will help you understand the pros and cons of each option based on your body type, aesthetic goals, and lifestyle. Silicone implants generally offer a more natural feel and are preferred by most patients internationally. Saline implants may be appropriate for certain situations, including patients with very specific anatomical considerations.
