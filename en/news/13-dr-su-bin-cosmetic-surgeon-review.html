Who Is Dr. Su Bin

Dr. Su Bin (苏斌) is a key member of the surgical team at Anhe Meige in Shanghai. As a director-level cosmetic surgeon, Dr. Su brings significant experience to the clinic's medical practice. Alongside Dr. Hu Zhi, Dr. Su Bin forms part of the core surgical team that international patients encounter when choosing Anhe Meige for their cosmetic procedures. This profile provides objective information about Dr. Su's background and approach to help prospective patients make informed decisions about their care.

Professional Background and Credentials

Dr. Su Bin is a registered medical practitioner in China with qualifications in plastic and cosmetic surgery. His professional credentials are verifiable through China's medical practitioner registration system, and Anhe Meige maintains transparency about his qualifications. Dr. Su's training encompasses both foundational surgical skills and specialized aesthetic techniques. His registration with the appropriate medical authorities confirms that he meets the professional standards required to perform cosmetic surgical procedures at Anhe Meige.

Areas of Surgical Focus

At Anhe Meige, Dr. Su Bin's surgical practice encompasses a range of cosmetic procedures with particular emphasis on facial aesthetics. His expertise includes procedures such as double eyelid surgery, facial fat grafting, and facial contouring. Patients who have consulted with Dr. Su describe his approach as measured and thoughtful, with careful attention to achieving results that look natural rather than overdone. He is known for spending adequate time during consultations to understand patient goals and explain the surgical approach in detail. This thoroughness aligns with Anhe Meige's commitment to patient education and informed consent.

Patient Perspectives on Dr. Su Bin

Patient feedback about Dr. Su Bin at Anhe Meige tends to emphasize his patient communication style and meticulous approach. International patients note that through the clinic's interpreter services, they are able to have meaningful conversations with Dr. Su about their goals and concerns. Reviews mention that he is straightforward about what procedures can realistically achieve and does not oversell additional treatments. Some patients have specifically chosen Dr. Su after comparing multiple surgeons, citing his detailed consultation style and willingness to answer extensive questions as deciding factors.
