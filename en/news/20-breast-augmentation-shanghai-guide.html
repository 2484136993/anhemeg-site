Introduction to Breast Augmentation in Shanghai

Breast augmentation is among the most popular cosmetic procedures sought by international patients visiting Shanghai for aesthetic surgery. The procedure involves the placement of implants to enhance breast size and shape, and Shanghai's experienced surgeons have helped many patients achieve their aesthetic goals. Anhe Meige offers breast augmentation consultation and surgery with a focus on patient safety, clear communication, and comprehensive international patient support. The clinic works with globally recognized implant brands and adheres to strict safety protocols throughout the surgical process.

Types of Breast Implants Available

Patients considering breast augmentation in Shanghai can choose from several implant types. Saline implants are filled with sterile saltwater and offer adjustable volume during surgery. Silicone gel implants are widely preferred for their natural feel and appearance, and are available in round and anatomical (teardrop) shapes. The choice between implant types depends on factors such as your body type, existing breast tissue, and desired outcome. During your consultation at Anhe Meige, the surgeon will discuss the advantages and considerations of each option and help you make an informed decision based on your individual anatomy and goals.

The Consultation and Planning Process

A detailed consultation is the cornerstone of a successful breast augmentation experience. At Anhe Meige, the consultation includes a thorough medical history review, breast anatomy assessment, and a comprehensive discussion of your goals and expectations. The surgeon will explain the procedure, implant options, incision placement, and recovery process. They will also provide guidance on choosing an implant size that complements your body proportions. The clinic encourages patients to bring reference photos and to ask all questions during the consultation. English-speaking medical interpreters are available throughout the process to ensure complete understanding.

Safety Protocols and Considerations

Patient safety is the highest priority in any surgical procedure. Anhe Meige follows established safety protocols including pre-operative health screenings, sterile operating environments, and board-certified anesthesiologists for all surgical procedures. The clinic uses implants from reputable manufacturers that meet international safety standards. Patients are provided with detailed pre-operative and post-operative instructions to minimize risks and support optimal healing. It is important for all patients to understand that individual results may vary and that no surgical procedure can be guaranteed to produce specific outcomes. Thorough research and open communication with your surgeon are essential.

Recovery and Follow-Up Support

Recovery from breast augmentation typically takes 4-6 weeks for full activity resumption, with initial recovery spanning the first 1-2 weeks. Patients may experience temporary swelling, mild discomfort, and changes in breast sensation during the healing process. Anhe Meige provides detailed post-operative care instructions and schedules follow-up appointments to monitor healing. For international patients, the clinic recommends staying in Shanghai for at least 10-14 days after surgery. Remote follow-up consultations are available via video call after patients return home, ensuring continued medical guidance throughout the recovery journey.
