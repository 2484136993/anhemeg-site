How to Prepare for Your Aesthetic Consultation in Shanghai

Anhe Meige · Medical Aesthetic Consultation Platform

Home/ Articles

Documentation Preparation

Bring valid identification documents and any relevant medical history records. Having this information ready helps streamline the registration process.

Understanding Your Goals

Before your visit, take time to clearly define your aesthetic goals and expectations. Understanding what you hope to achieve helps our consultants provide better guidance.

Questions to Consider

Consider preparing questions about the consultation process, expected outcomes, and recovery considerations. Writing them down ensures you don't forget important points.

Travel Arrangement Tips

Plan your Shanghai visit with sufficient time for consultation and any follow-up appointments. We recommend staying at least 2-3 days for comprehensive consultations.
