Overview of Body Contouring in Shanghai

Body contouring encompasses a range of surgical and non-surgical procedures designed to reshape and improve the appearance of the body. Popular body contouring procedures in Shanghai include liposuction, tummy tuck (abdominoplasty), arm lifts, thigh lifts, and non-invasive fat reduction treatments. International patients increasingly choose Shanghai for body contouring due to the combination of skilled surgeons, modern facilities, and competitive pricing. Anhe Meige provides body contouring consultation and procedures with comprehensive international patient support, ensuring that language barriers and logistical challenges do not impede access to quality care.

Liposuction Options and Techniques

Liposuction remains one of the most commonly requested body contouring procedures. Modern liposuction techniques available in Shanghai include tumescent liposuction, which uses a specialized solution to reduce bleeding and improve fat removal precision, and power-assisted liposuction (PAL), which uses vibrating cannulas for more efficient fat extraction. Laser-assisted liposuction and ultrasound-assisted liposuction are also available, offering additional skin tightening benefits. The choice of technique depends on the treatment area, volume of fat to be removed, and the patient's skin quality. During consultation at Anhe Meige, the surgeon will assess your body composition and recommend the most appropriate technique for your specific needs.

Tummy Tuck and Abdominal Contouring

Tummy tuck surgery, or abdominoplasty, addresses excess skin and weakened abdominal muscles that cannot be improved through diet and exercise alone. This procedure is particularly sought after by patients who have experienced significant weight loss or pregnancy. Full abdominoplasty involves tightening the abdominal muscles and removing excess skin from the upper and lower abdomen, while mini-abdominoplasty focuses on the lower abdomen below the navel. At Anhe Meige, the surgeon will evaluate your abdominal anatomy and discuss which approach best suits your condition and goals, providing honest guidance about expected outcomes and recovery requirements.

Non-Invasive Body Contouring Alternatives

For patients seeking body contouring without surgery, several non-invasive options are available. Cryolipolysis (fat freezing) selectively reduces fat in targeted areas by freezing fat cells, which are then naturally eliminated by the body. Radiofrequency treatments use thermal energy to tighten skin and reduce localized fat. These non-surgical options are suitable for patients with mild to moderate fat deposits who prefer minimal downtime, though the results are generally more subtle compared to surgical options. Anhe Meige offers both surgical and non-surgical body contouring treatments, and the medical team provides guidance on which approach aligns with your goals, anatomy, and lifestyle preferences.

Recovery and Long-Term Considerations

Recovery from body contouring procedures varies significantly depending on the specific treatment. Liposuction typically requires 1-2 weeks of initial recovery, with compression garments worn for 4-6 weeks. Tummy tuck recovery is more extensive, usually requiring 2-4 weeks before returning to normal activities and 6-8 weeks before resuming exercise. Maintaining a stable weight through healthy diet and regular exercise is essential for preserving body contouring results long-term. Anhe Meige provides detailed recovery guidelines, schedules follow-up appointments, and offers remote consultation support for international patients after they return home, ensuring continuity of care throughout the healing process.
