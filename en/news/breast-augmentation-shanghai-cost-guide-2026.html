Understanding Breast Augmentation: Types of Procedures

Before discussing costs, it's important to understand the different types of breast augmentation procedures available in Shanghai. The choice of procedure significantly impacts the overall cost and expected outcomes.

Implant-Based Augmentation

This is the most common form of breast augmentation worldwide, and Shanghai's clinics offer the full range of implant options:

  • Saline implants: Filled with sterile salt water after insertion. Less commonly chosen in China, as they tend to feel less natural than silicone options. Available but not the primary choice.
  • Cohesive silicone gel implants: The most popular choice in Shanghai and across China. These "gummy bear" implants maintain their shape even if the shell is compromised. Brands commonly used include Mentor (Johnson & Johnson, USA), Motiva (Establishment Labs, USA/Europe), and domestic Chinese brands like Guangzhou Wanhe that have obtained NMPA (National Medical Products Administration) certification.
  • Anatomical (teardrop) vs. round implants: Anatomical implants provide a more natural slope, while round implants offer more fullness in the upper pole. Shanghai surgeons are experienced with both shapes and will recommend based on your body type and aesthetic goals.

Fat Transfer Breast Augmentation

Also known as "autologous fat grafting," this technique uses your own body fat (harvested via liposuction from areas like the abdomen or thighs) to enhance breast volume. This option is gaining popularity in Shanghai for patients seeking a more modest increase (typically one cup size) with the added benefit of body contouring at the donor site.

Fat transfer is particularly suitable for patients who want a natural look and feel, are uncomfortable with synthetic implants, or desire simultaneous body sculpting. However, it's important to note that fat transfer may require multiple sessions to achieve the desired volume, as some of the transferred fat may be reabsorbed by the body.

Composite Augmentation

A combination approach using both implants and fat transfer, composite augmentation is increasingly offered by Shanghai's leading clinics. This technique provides the volume enhancement of implants with the natural feel and improved upper pole coverage of fat grafting. It's particularly beneficial for patients with thin natural breast tissue who want to avoid visible implant edges.

Choosing the Right Surgeon in Shanghai

Selecting the right surgeon is arguably the most critical decision in your breast augmentation journey. Here's a comprehensive framework for evaluating surgeons in Shanghai:

Credentials to Verify

  • Medical license: Confirm the surgeon holds a valid Physician Practice Certificate (医师执业证书) registered in Shanghai
  • Specialist certification: Look for certification from the Chinese Society of Plastic and Aesthetic Surgery (中华医学会整形外科学分会)
  • Experience volume: Ask how many breast augmentation procedures the surgeon performs annually — a high-volume surgeon (100+ per year) typically delivers more consistent results
  • Before-and-after portfolio: Review at least 20-30 cases that are similar to your body type and desired outcome
  • International patient experience: Surgeons accustomed to working with international patients will better understand cross-cultural aesthetic preferences

Key Questions to Ask During Consultation

  1. What implant brands and types do you recommend for my body type, and why?
  2. What incision approach do you suggest (inframammary, periareolar, or transaxillary) and what are the trade-offs?
  3. What is your complication rate, and how do you handle revisions if needed?
  4. Can I see 3D simulation of the expected outcome before committing?
  5. What is included in your price, and what additional costs should I anticipate?
  6. What does your post-operative care protocol look like for international patients?

The Complete Patient Journey: What to Expect

Pre-Trip Planning (4-8 weeks before)

Begin your journey with an online consultation. Most leading Shanghai clinics, including Anhe Meige, offer free or low-cost virtual consultations where you can share your goals, receive preliminary recommendations, and get a detailed cost estimate. This is also the time to arrange your visa (China offers various visa types depending on your nationality), flights, and accommodation.

Arrival & Pre-Operative Phase (Day 1-2)

Upon arrival in Shanghai, you'll have your first in-person consultation. This typically includes a thorough health assessment, measurements, 3D imaging simulation, and detailed discussion of your options. Pre-operative medical tests (blood work, ECG, chest X-ray) are conducted to ensure you're fit for surgery. Most clinics schedule surgery for the day after consultation, though some patients prefer an extra day to settle in.

Surgery Day (Day 2-3)

Breast augmentation surgery typically takes 1.5-3 hours depending on the complexity. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia with a dedicated anesthesiologist monitoring your vital signs throughout. Most patients are able to go home or to their hotel the same day, though some clinics recommend one night of observation.

Acute Recovery Phase (Days 3-10)

The first week is the most critical for recovery. Expect swelling, bruising, and some discomfort, all of which are manageable with prescribed medication. You'll need to wear a surgical bra continuously and avoid raising your arms above shoulder level. Most patients feel comfortable enough for light sightseeing by day 5-7, though strenuous activity should be avoided.

Early Recovery (Weeks 2-6)

By the second week, most visible bruising has subsided and you'll start seeing the initial shape. Stitches are typically removed around day 10-14. You can gradually resume light activities, but should continue to avoid heavy lifting and vigorous exercise. Your surgeon will schedule follow-up appointments to monitor healing progress.

Long-Term Recovery (Months 2-6)

Implants gradually settle into their final position over the first 3-6 months. Scars continue to fade and soften. By the 6-month mark, your results will be fully stabilized. International patients can maintain contact with their surgeon through video follow-up consultations during this period.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should I plan to stay in Shanghai for breast augmentation?

We recommend a minimum of 10-14 days: 1-2 days for consultation and pre-op tests, 1 day for surgery, and 7-10 days for initial recovery before flying. Your surgeon will advise when it's safe to fly based on your individual recovery progress.

Are breast implants safe? What about BIA-ALCL?

Modern silicone implants are considered very safe by major health authorities worldwide. BIA-ALCL (Breast Implant-Associated Anaplastic Large Cell Lymphoma) is an extremely rare condition associated primarily with textured-surface implants. Leading Shanghai surgeons use smooth or micro-textured implants from reputable manufacturers (Mentor, Motiva) that have excellent safety profiles. Your surgeon will discuss the risk-benefit profile during consultation.

Will I need to replace my implants in the future?

Modern implants are designed to be long-lasting but are not considered "lifetime devices." Most manufacturers offer warranties of 10-20 years. Many patients keep their implants indefinitely without issues, but periodic monitoring (MRI or ultrasound) is recommended. If replacement is needed, Shanghai's surgeons can handle revision procedures.

Can I breastfeed after breast augmentation?

In most cases, yes. Modern surgical techniques, particularly the inframammary incision approach, are designed to preserve milk ducts and glandular tissue. However, individual results vary, and some women may experience reduced milk production. Discuss your family planning goals with your surgeon during consultation.

What if I'm not satisfied with the results?

Reputable clinics have clear revision policies. Minor asymmetries or adjustments are relatively common and can usually be addressed with a minor procedure. At established clinics like Anhe Meige, the surgeon will work with you to achieve the best possible outcome, and many revision procedures are covered within the first year at no additional surgical cost (implant costs may apply).

How do I choose the right implant size?

Your surgeon will take precise measurements of your chest wall dimensions, existing breast tissue, skin elasticity, and body proportions to recommend an appropriate size range. The final decision is always yours, but a good surgeon will guide you toward a size that looks natural and proportionate for your body. 3D simulation technology can help you visualize different size options before making your decision.

What makes Shanghai different from other Asian destinations like Seoul or Bangkok?

Shanghai offers a unique combination: the technical sophistication and safety standards of a top-tier global city, at pricing that's 40-60% below Western countries. Compared to Seoul, Shanghai offers similar quality with less aggressive upselling. Compared to Bangkok, Shanghai has more advanced technology and infrastructure. Additionally, Shanghai's large international community means the city is well-equipped to accommodate foreign visitors.
