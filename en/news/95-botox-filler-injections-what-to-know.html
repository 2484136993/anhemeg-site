Botox and dermal fillers are among the most popular non-surgical aesthetic treatments. Here's what you should know before scheduling a consultation.

Understanding Injectables

Injectable treatments like Botox and fillers can address various aesthetic concerns. Anhe Meige provides consultation services to help you understand these options.

Key Differences

Botox and fillers work differently: Botox relaxes muscles to reduce wrinkles, fillers add volume to specific areas, results and longevity vary, and touch-up schedules differ.

Consultation Importance

A thorough consultation is essential before any injectable treatment. Anhe Meige ensures you have all the information needed to make an informed decision.
