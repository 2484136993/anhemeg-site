Preoperative Preparation and Recovery Considerations

Successful surgical outcomes depend significantly on proper preparation before the procedure and appropriate care during recovery. Patients scheduled for procedures with Dr. Su Bin receive detailed preoperative instructions to help ensure optimal conditions for surgery.

Prior to surgery, patients typically undergo health screening to verify that they are suitable candidates for the planned procedure. Disclosure of complete medical history, including any chronic conditions or medications being taken, enables appropriate planning and risk assessment. Certain medications and supplements that may increase bleeding risk are typically discontinued before surgery, following specific guidelines provided by the medical team.

Lifestyle preparation in the days leading up to surgery includes avoiding tobacco products and limiting alcohol consumption. Adequate rest and stress management contribute to overall wellbeing during the preoperative period. Patients should arrange their schedule to accommodate appropriate recovery time, including assistance with daily activities during the immediate postoperative period if needed.

The recovery experience varies depending on the specific procedure performed. Following eye surgery, temporary swelling and bruising around the surgical site are normal and typically resolve over one to two weeks. Patients receive specific instructions regarding wound care, activity restrictions, and signs that might indicate complications requiring medical attention. Adherence to these postoperative guidelines significantly influences the final aesthetic outcome and overall surgical experience.
