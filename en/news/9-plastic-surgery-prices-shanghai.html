Understanding Shanghai Plastic Surgery Pricing

One of the primary reasons international patients choose Shanghai for cosmetic surgery is the significant cost advantage compared to other medical tourism destinations. Plastic surgery prices in Shanghai are typically 40–60% lower than equivalent procedures in South Korea, 50–70% lower than in Japan, and up to 80% lower than in Western countries. However, understanding the pricing structure is essential for accurate budget planning. This guide provides general price ranges and explains the factors that influence costs at clinics like Anhe Meige.

General Price Ranges for Popular Procedures

The following price ranges reflect the Shanghai market as of 2025 and are provided for reference only. Actual prices vary by clinic, surgeon experience, and individual case complexity. Double eyelid surgery (blepharoplasty) typically ranges from ¥6,000 to ¥15,000 (approximately $800–$2,100). Rhinoplasty ranges from ¥15,000 to ¥50,000 ($2,100–$7,000) depending on the technique and materials used. V-line jaw contouring ranges from ¥30,000 to ¥80,000 ($4,200–$11,200). Breast augmentation ranges from ¥25,000 to ¥70,000 ($3,500–$9,800). Liposuction ranges from ¥10,000 to ¥40,000 ($1,400–$5,600) per area. These prices at Anhe Meige include standard pre-operative assessment and basic follow-up care.

Factors That Affect Plastic Surgery Pricing in Shanghai

Several factors influence the final cost of your procedure. Surgeon experience and reputation significantly impact pricing — senior surgeons with extensive track records command higher fees. The complexity of your specific case, including anatomical considerations and whether revision surgery is needed, affects cost. The type of anesthesia (local vs. general) and the duration of the procedure also factor in. Implant materials or injectable products used represent another variable. Additionally, international patient services such as interpreter support, airport transfers, and extended follow-up may be included in package pricing or offered as add-ons. Anhe Meige provides detailed cost breakdowns during consultation.

Budget Planning for Your Medical Tourism Trip

When budgeting for plastic surgery in Shanghai, consider costs beyond the procedure itself. Travel expenses including flights can range from $300 to $1,500 depending on your origin. Accommodation near the clinic typically costs $40–$120 per night. Plan for 7–14 days of stay for most procedures. Meals and local transportation add approximately $30–$60 per day. Visa fees vary by nationality. Even with these additional costs, the total expense is typically 40–60% less than having the same procedure in Korea and 60–80% less than in Western countries. Anhe Meige can provide estimated total cost projections including recommended accommodation and travel guidance.

Comparing Shanghai Prices With Other Destinations

To put Shanghai's pricing in perspective, consider these approximate comparisons for rhinoplasty: Shanghai $2,100–$7,000, Seoul $3,500–$12,000, Bangkok $2,500–$8,000, Tokyo $5,000–$15,000, Sydney $6,000–$18,000, London $5,000–$15,000, and New York $7,000–$20,000. Shanghai offers competitive pricing while maintaining high medical standards in licensed facilities.
