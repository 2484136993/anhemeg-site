Arrival and Registration

International patients are welcomed at Anhe Meige's reception area. Our staff assists with registration and ensures a smooth check-in process.

Consultation Environment

Consultations take place in private, comfortable spaces designed for confidential discussions. The environment promotes open communication between patients and consultants.

Discussion and Assessment

During the consultation, our team conducts thorough assessments and engages in detailed discussions. Patients are encouraged to share their thoughts and ask questions freely.

Departure and Next Steps

Before departure, patients receive comprehensive information about recommended services and next steps. Our team explains available options and answers all questions.
