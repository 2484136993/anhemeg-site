Visa and Travel Documents

International patients should ensure they have valid passports and appropriate visas for travel to China. A tourist visa is typically sufficient for consultation visits.

Shanghai Accommodation Options

Shanghai offers various accommodation options near Anhe Meige, ranging from budget-friendly hotels to luxury properties. The Hongqiao area provides convenient access to the clinic.

Local Transportation

Shanghai's metro system offers efficient transportation throughout the city. Taxis and ride-hailing services are also readily available for convenient travel.

Trip Planning Timeline

We recommend planning your trip at least 2-3 weeks in advance. This allows sufficient time for visa processing, accommodation booking, and appointment scheduling.
