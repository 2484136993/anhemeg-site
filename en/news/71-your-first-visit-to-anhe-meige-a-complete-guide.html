Before Your Visit

International patients can contact Anhe Meige via WeChat or phone to schedule consultations in advance. Early booking helps ensure convenient appointment times.

What to Prepare

We recommend preparing a list of questions and understanding your basic aesthetic goals before the consultation. Bring your identification documents as registration may be required.

During Your Consultation

During your visit, our consultants will conduct a thorough assessment and provide professional recommendations. Please feel free to ask questions about any concerns you may have.

After Your Visit

After your consultation, you will receive detailed information about the discussed services. Take time to consider your options before making any decisions.
