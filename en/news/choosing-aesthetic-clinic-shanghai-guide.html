Factors to Consider When Choosing a Clinic

Selecting the right aesthetic clinic is crucial for a positive experience. Here are key factors to consider when choosing a clinic in Shanghai.

Key Evaluation Criteria

  • Professional credentials and certifications
  • Transparency in services and pricing
  • Reviews and testimonials from previous clients
  • Communication in your preferred language

Questions to Ask

Before committing to any service, ask about the consultation process, aftercare support, and what to expect during your visit.
