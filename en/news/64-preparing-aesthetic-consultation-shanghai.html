Preparing for Your Aesthetic Consultation in Shanghai

Anhe Meige May 27, 2026

Proper preparation is key to maximizing the value of your aesthetic consultation. Whether you're considering your first procedure or exploring additional treatments, taking time to prepare ensures productive discussions and clear understanding of your options.

Before Your Consultation

Begin by clearly defining your aesthetic goals. Consider what changes you'd like to see, what concerns you'd like to address, and what outcomes you're hoping to achieve. Having a clear vision helps guide productive conversations with the Anhe Meige team.

Gather any relevant medical information, including previous procedures, allergies, current medications, and general health conditions. This information is important for providing safe, appropriate guidance.

Research and Questions

Prepare a list of questions you'd like to ask during the consultation. Common questions include those about procedure options, recovery times, potential risks, and cost considerations. Anhe Meige welcomes all questions and provides thorough, honest answers.

Research basic information about the procedures you're considering. While Anhe Meige provides comprehensive explanations, having some background knowledge helps ensure productive discussions.

Setting Realistic Expectations

Understanding what aesthetic procedures can and cannot achieve is crucial. Anhe Meige emphasizes realistic expectations, helping patients understand potential outcomes based on their individual circumstances. This approach leads to greater satisfaction with results.

Discuss your expectations openly with the team. If your goals may not align with realistic outcomes, Anhe Meige provides honest guidance and suggests alternative approaches that might better suit your needs.

Logistical Preparation

For in-person consultations in Shanghai, plan your travel and accommodation arrangements in advance. Anhe Meige can provide recommendations for convenient locations and assist with coordination as needed.
