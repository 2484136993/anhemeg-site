What Does 'Licensed Clinic' Mean in China's Medical System

In China, a licensed medical aesthetics clinic must obtain a Medical Institution Practice License from the local health commission. This is not a simple registration — it requires passing comprehensive inspections of the facility's medical equipment, safety systems, staffing qualifications, infection control protocols, and emergency procedures. The license must be renewed periodically, and clinics are subject to unannounced inspections. When we say Anhe Meige is a licensed clinic, it means the facility has met and continues to meet these rigorous standards set by Shanghai's health authorities.

The Regulatory Framework Governing Shanghai's Cosmetic Surgery Industry

Shanghai's cosmetic surgery industry is governed by multiple layers of regulation. The National Health Commission sets the overall framework, while the Shanghai Municipal Health Commission enforces local standards. Additional oversight comes from the National Medical Products Administration for any implants or medical devices used. Clinics must comply with regulations covering practitioner qualifications, informed consent protocols, medical record keeping, and adverse event reporting. Anhe Meige operates in full compliance with all applicable regulations, which is a baseline requirement for patient safety.

Why Licensing Matters for International Patients

For international patients, clinic licensing provides several important protections. Licensed clinics are required to maintain proper medical records, which means your treatment history is documented and accessible. They must carry medical liability insurance, providing recourse in the unlikely event of complications. Their facilities meet minimum safety standards, including emergency equipment and protocols. Their practitioners are verified professionals with legitimate qualifications. Choosing a licensed clinic like Anhe Meige means these protections are in place, giving you significantly more security than an unlicensed provider could offer.

Understanding the Different Types of Medical Licenses in China

China's medical licensing system distinguishes between different levels of facilities. Medical beauty clinics (医疗美容门诊部) like Anhe Meige are licensed to perform specific categories of cosmetic procedures. This is different from general outpatient clinics or beauty salons, which are not permitted to perform surgical procedures. Understanding these distinctions is important because some unscrupulous operators attempt to perform surgical procedures without the appropriate license class. Anhe Meige holds the correct license category for the cosmetic surgery procedures it offers, and this can be verified through official channels.

Questions to Ask Any Shanghai Clinic About Their License

Before committing to any clinic in Shanghai, consider asking these questions: Can I see your Medical Institution Practice License? What is your license number and issuing authority? Which categories of procedures is your license approved for? Are your surgeons registered with the National Health Commission? Can you provide proof of medical liability insurance? How often are you inspected by health authorities? A legitimate, licensed clinic like Anhe Meige will answer all these questions readily and may even proactively provide this information.
