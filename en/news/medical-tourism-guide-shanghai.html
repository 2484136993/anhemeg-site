Medical Tourism Guide Shanghai 2026 - Complete International Patient Handbook

Anhe Meige | Medical Tourism | Updated: June 2026

Why Shanghai Is Becoming a Top Medical Tourism Destination

The Rise of Shanghai's Medical Aesthetics Industry

Over the past decade, Shanghai has transformed into a hub for medical aesthetics, with numerous internationally recognized aesthetic clinics establishing their presence in the city. Major aesthetic institutions employ cutting-edge technology and maintain rigorous standards of hygiene and patient safety.

The regulatory environment in China has also matured significantly. The National Health Commission enforces strict licensing requirements, ensuring that only qualified professionals perform aesthetic procedures.

Cost Advantages: Why Patients Choose Shanghai

One of the most compelling reasons international patients choose Shanghai for aesthetic procedures is the significant cost advantage. On average, cosmetic surgeries in Shanghai cost 50-70% less than in the United States, United Kingdom, or Australia, while maintaining comparable quality standards.

Price comparisons for common aesthetic procedures:

  • Rhinoplasty: $1,500-4,000 in Shanghai vs. $5,000-15,000 in Western countries
  • Facelift: $3,000-6,000 in Shanghai vs. $10,000-20,000 in Western countries
  • Breast Augmentation: $2,500-5,000 in Shanghai vs. $6,000-15,000 in Western countries
  • Double Eyelid Surgery: $800-2,000 in Shanghai vs. $3,000-8,000 in Western countries

Accessibility and Convenience

Shanghai's position as a major international transportation hub makes it exceptionally accessible. The city is served by two major international airports offering direct flights from most major cities worldwide.

Essential Pre-Travel Planning

Understanding Visa Requirements

China offers a Medical Visa (M-Visa) specifically designed for individuals seeking medical treatment in the country. Required documents include valid passport, completed visa application, invitation letter from your chosen medical institution, and proof of medical appointment.

The M-Visa application typically takes 4-7 business days to process. Expedited processing is available for additional fees.

Remote Consultation: Your First Step

Before traveling to Shanghai, we strongly recommend scheduling a remote consultation with your chosen clinic. Benefits include receiving a preliminary assessment, understanding estimated costs and timeline, and building rapport with your surgeon.

Prepare clear photographs of the area you wish to treat, a detailed description of your goals, medical history, and questions for the surgeon.

Creating Your Medical Travel Timeline

Different procedures require different recovery periods:

  • Non-surgical treatments: 1-2 days in Shanghai
  • Minor surgical procedures: 3-5 days in Shanghai
  • Moderate procedures: 7-10 days in Shanghai
  • Major procedures: 10-14 days in Shanghai
  • Complex surgeries: 14-21 days in Shanghai

Arriving in Shanghai: Transportation and Accommodation

Getting from the Airport

Pudong International Airport (PVG): Located approximately 40 km east of central Shanghai. Options include Maglev train (8 minutes to city, ¥50), Metro Line 2 (60-90 minutes), taxi (¥150-250).

Hongqiao International Airport (SHA): Located approximately 13 km west of central Shanghai. Well-connected to city center via Metro Line 2 and taxi services.

Navigating Shanghai

Shanghai's public transportation network is among the most efficient in Asia. The metro system comprises 19 lines covering virtually every corner of the city with English signage. Download Didi (滴滴出行) for convenient ride-hailing services.

Choosing Accommodation

Many aesthetic clinics are concentrated in central Shanghai districts such as Huangpu, Jing'an, Xuhui, and Changning. Options include luxury hotels (¥1,000+/night), boutique hotels (¥400-1,000/night), and serviced apartments (¥300-600/night) ideal for longer stays.

The Medical Experience: What to Expect

Making an Appointment

Reputable Shanghai clinics operate on an appointment-only basis. Book via WeChat (kyt3158), email, or the clinic's international patient hotline. Most clinics request appointment confirmation at least 48 hours in advance.

Your First Visit

Expect reception and registration, medical consultation with your surgeon, cost estimation, and decision time. Reputable clinics never pressure patients into immediate decisions.

Pre-Surgery Preparation

If you decide to proceed with surgery, you will receive medical evaluation, pre-operative instructions, and complete consent and documentation. Fast from midnight before surgery and stop blood-thinning medications as directed.

Surgery Day

Arrive 1-2 hours before scheduled surgery. The process includes admission, pre-op assessment, anesthesia consultation, final confirmation, surgery, and recovery. Most aesthetic surgeries are performed under general anesthesia or deep sedation.

Recovery and Aftercare

Hospital Discharge and Immediate Post-Op

Following most aesthetic surgeries, you will be discharged the same day after a few hours of observation. Ensure you have detailed post-operative instructions, medication prescriptions, follow-up schedule, and emergency contact numbers.

Recovery Guidelines

Facial Procedures: Keep head elevated, apply cold compresses, avoid strenuous activities, protect from direct sunlight.

Body Procedures: Wear compression garments as instructed, avoid soaking surgical sites, take gentle walks to promote circulation.

Follow-Up Appointments

Reputable clinics offer scheduled follow-ups: Day 3-5 for initial check, Day 7-10 for stitch removal, Week 4 for mid-recovery evaluation, and Month 3/6 for final assessment. Remote aftercare options are available for patients who cannot extend their Shanghai stay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Language and Communication

Shanghai's leading aesthetic clinics employ English-speaking coordinators and medical staff. Translation services for other languages are also available.

Payment and Costs

Most clinics accept cash (RMB), major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), and bank transfers. Reputable clinics provide transparent pricing with no hidden costs.

Safety and Quality

Look for government-issued medical institution licenses, verified surgeon qualifications, international certifications (JCI, ISO), and positive reviews from verified international patients.

Making an Informed Decision

Research thoroughly, verify clinic credentials, understand the procedure, and plan for contingencies. Purchase comprehensive travel insurance and have emergency funds available.

Red Flags to Avoid: Clinics that promise guaranteed results, quote prices significantly below market rates, pressure you into immediate decisions, or lack proper licensing.
