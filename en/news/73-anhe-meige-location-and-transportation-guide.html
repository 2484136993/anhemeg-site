Clinic Address

Anhe Meige is located at Building 4, No. 38 Xuxiang Road, Xujing Town, Qingpu District, Shanghai. The clinic sits in the heart of the Hongqiao Medical Circle.

From Shanghai Hongqiao

From Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station or Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2, you can take a taxi or bus to reach our clinic. The journey typically takes 15-20 minutes by car.

From Shanghai Pudong Airport

From Shanghai Pudong International Airport, the clinic is approximately 60 kilometers away. Consider taking the Maglev train to Longyang Road, then metro or taxi to Hongqiao area.

Nearby Accommodations

Anhe Meige is conveniently located near several hotels and commercial complexes including Hongqiao Tiandi and Longfor Tianjie. We recommend booking accommodations in advance.
