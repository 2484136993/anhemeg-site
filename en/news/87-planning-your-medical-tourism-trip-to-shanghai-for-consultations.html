Trip Planning Timeline

Begin planning your Shanghai consultation trip at least 4-6 weeks in advance. This allows sufficient time for visa processing and accommodation booking.

Documentation Requirements

Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned travel dates. Check if you need a visa for China based on your nationality.

Shanghai City Information

Shanghai offers world-class infrastructure and amenities. The city is well-connected with modern transportation systems and diverse accommodation options.

Post-Consultation Options

After your consultation, consider exploring Shanghai's attractions if time permits. Our team can provide recommendations for local sightseeing and travel tips.
