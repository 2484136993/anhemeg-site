❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Top Cosmetic Surgery Clinic Shanghai

How much does cosmetic surgery cost in Shanghai? +

Costs vary by procedure type and complexity. Generally, cosmetic surgery in Shanghai costs 40-60% less than in Korea or Western countries. We provide a detailed quote after your consultation based on your specific needs and goals.

Do I need to speak Chinese? +

No. Anhe Meige provides English-speaking medical interpreters during all consultations and procedures. Our support team is also available via WhatsApp and WeChat in English 24/7.

How long do I need to stay in Shanghai? +

Most patients stay 5-7 days. Minimally invasive procedures (like injectables) may require only 1-2 days. Surgical procedures (like rhinoplasty, V-line) typically require 5-7 days for initial recovery and follow-up check-ups before travel.

Is cosmetic surgery in Shanghai safe? +

Anhe Meige follows strict medical safety protocols with standard monitoring before, during, and after all procedures. Our surgeons are experienced professionals with international training. We provide comprehensive pre-procedure health assessments for all patients.

Can I see before and after photos? +

Yes, we can share relevant reference photos during your personal consultation to help you understand potential outcomes. Please note that results vary by individual and these photos are for reference purposes only.

What happens after I return home? +

We provide remote follow-up support for up to 6 months after your procedure. This includes scheduled video consultations, photo review, and 24/7 messaging support for any recovery questions. Visit our Aftercare page for details.

Do you help with visa applications? +

While we are not a visa agency, we provide support documents including clinic introduction letters, hotel booking confirmations, and step-by-step application guidance. Visit our Visa Guide for more information.

What if I'm not satisfied with the results? +

We encourage thorough pre-procedure consultations to align expectations. Results may vary based on individual factors. We provide revision consultation if needed. Our team is committed to working with you throughout the entire process to address any concerns.
