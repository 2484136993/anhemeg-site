Understanding the Recovery Process

Recovery after cosmetic surgery is a crucial factor in determining your final results. At Anhe Meige, we provide detailed guidance and free remote follow-up to ensure you have the best possible recovery experience.

📅 Recovery Timeline

Day 1 — Right After Surgery

First Day

Complete rest at the clinic. Apply gentle cold compresses to reduce swelling. Take pain medication and antibiotics as prescribed. Keep your head elevated above your heart when sleeping. Avoid strenuous activity and do not touch the surgical area.

Days 2-7

First Week

Swelling and bruising peak on days 2-3, then gradually subside. Light walking around your room is encouraged. Follow wound care instructions from the nursing staff. Eat soft foods, avoid spicy and hot dishes. You may shower but avoid getting the wound wet. Avoid direct sunlight.

Weeks 2-4

First Month

Significant swelling reduction, initial results become visible. You can resume light daily activities and take short walks outdoors. Let scabs fall off naturally (do not pick). Attend scheduled follow-up appointments. Light makeup may be worn after 2 weeks (varies by procedure).

Months 2-3

Three Months Post-Surgery

Results stabilize, swelling fully resolved. You can resume normal exercise routines. Scars gradually fade over time. Final results are clear and natural-looking. Continue monitoring and attend follow-ups as needed.

📋 Procedure-Specific Recovery Tips

👁️

Double Eyelid Surgery

  • Quick recovery: 5-7 days
  • Cold compress 48h, then warm compress
  • No eye rubbing, avoid bending head down
  • Use prescribed eye drops
  • Avoid contact lenses for 2 weeks
👃

S-line Rhinoplasty

  • Recovery: 7-14 days
  • No glasses for 4-6 weeks
  • Limit bending down, avoid strenuous activity
  • Rinse mouth with saline solution
  • Protect nose from impact
🦴

V-line Jaw Contouring

  • Recovery: 14-30 days
  • Wear compression bandage as directed
  • Soft food diet for 2-3 weeks
  • Avoid wide mouth opening
  • Gentle massage after 2 weeks
👙

Breast Augmentation

  • Recovery: 14-30 days
  • Wear surgical bra 4-6 weeks
  • No raising arms high for 2 weeks
  • Sleep on back with pillows elevated
  • Avoid upper body exercise for 6 weeks
💨

Liposuction

  • Recovery: 7-21 days
  • Wear compression garment 4-6 weeks
  • Light walking within 24 hours
  • Massage treated area after 2 weeks
  • Stay hydrated, eat lightly
💉

Filler / Botox Injections

  • Recovery: 1-3 days
  • No touching/massaging area for 24h
  • Avoid sun, sauna for 1 week
  • No strenuous exercise for 48h
  • Avoid sleeping face-down for 1 week

⚠️ When to Contact Your Doctor Immediately

Contact your doctor right away if you experience any of the following:

  • Increasing redness and swelling that doesn't improve after 72 hours
  • Fever above 38.5°C (101°F) lasting more than 24 hours
  • Severe pain not relieved by prescribed medication
  • Discharge or bleeding from the wound
  • Severe itching, rash, or allergic reaction
  • Difficulty breathing or chest pain (after breast augmentation)
