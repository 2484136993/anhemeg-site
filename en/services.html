👁️ Blepharoplasty Shanghai — Double Eyelid Surgery

Blepharoplasty in Shanghai creates a natural-looking crease in the upper eyelid, giving the eyes a more open, defined appearance. This is one of the most popular procedures for international patients visiting Shanghai for cosmetic surgery.

Who It's For

Patients with monolids, hooded eyelids, or asymmetrical eyelid creases who desire a more defined eye contour.

Recovery Time

Most patients return to normal activities within 7-10 days. Initial swelling subsides within the first week.

Travel Process

  • Online consultation before your trip to discuss your goals
  • 1-2 days in Shanghai for consultation and procedure
  • Post-procedure check-up before departure
  • Remote follow-up available after returning home
Note: Results may vary based on individual anatomy. This is a consultation service — please discuss your specific situation with our medical team.

👃 Rhinoplasty in Shanghai — Nose Surgery

Rhinoplasty in Shanghai at Anhe Meige focuses on creating a natural, harmonious nose shape. As one of the best facial plastic surgeons in Shanghai, our team uses autologous cartilage combined with safe implant materials for refined, elegant results.

Who It's For

Patients looking to enhance the nose bridge, refine the nasal tip, or improve overall facial balance.

Recovery Time

Splint removal at 7 days. Most swelling resolves within 10-14 days. Final results visible over several months.

Travel Process

  • Remote monitoring after returning home

💎 V-line Jaw Contouring

V-line jaw contouring reshapes the lower face to create a slim, refined V-shaped profile. This advanced procedure uses precise surgical techniques for natural-looking facial refinement.

Who It's For

Patients with a wide jaw, square face shape, or prominent jaw angles seeking a more delicate, contoured appearance.

Recovery Time

Initial recovery 14-21 days. Full results develop over 3-6 months as swelling gradually resolves.

Travel Process

  • Comprehensive pre-trip consultation including 3D imaging
  • 5-7 days in Shanghai for procedure and initial recovery
  • Regular remote follow-up sessions after departure
Note: Results may vary based on individual facial structure. This is a consultation-based service. No guaranteed outcomes.

🌸 Breast Augmentation Shanghai

Breast augmentation in Shanghai at Anhe Meige uses advanced endoscopic techniques for precise implant placement, resulting in a natural look and feel with minimal scarring.

Who It's For

Patients seeking to enhance breast size and shape, restore volume after pregnancy, or achieve better body proportions.

Recovery Time

Most patients resume light activities within 7-10 days. Full recovery typically takes 4-6 weeks.

Travel Process

  • Detailed online consultation to discuss implant options and expectations
  • 5-7 days in Shanghai for procedure and initial recovery monitoring
  • Ongoing remote follow-up for recovery guidance
Note: Results may vary. Implant options and sizing are discussed during personal consultation. No guaranteed outcomes.

🏋️ Liposuction Shanghai — Body Sculpting

Liposuction in Shanghai at Anhe Meige uses advanced techniques to target stubborn fat deposits. Minimally invasive approaches ensure faster recovery and smoother results.

Who It's For

Patients at a stable weight who want to remove localized fat deposits resistant to diet and exercise.

Recovery Time

Return to light activities in 5-7 days. Compression garments worn for 4-6 weeks for optimal results.

Travel Process

  • Online assessment of target areas and expectations
  • 3-5 days in Shanghai for procedure and monitoring
  • Remote guidance on compression and recovery protocols
Note: Results may vary. Liposuction is not a weight-loss solution. Please consult our team for personalized guidance.
