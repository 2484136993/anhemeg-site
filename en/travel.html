✈️ Flights to Shanghai

Shanghai is served by two major international airports with direct flights from cities worldwide:

Pudong International Airport (PVG)

Shanghai's primary international airport, serving most long-haul flights. Approximately 60 minutes by car from the city center.

  • From Asia: Tokyo (3h), Seoul (2h), Singapore (5h), Bangkok (4h), Manila (3h)
  • From Europe: London (12h), Frankfurt (11h), Paris (12h), Amsterdam (11h)
  • From Americas: Los Angeles (13h), New York (15h), Vancouver (12h), Sydney (10h)

Hongqiao International Airport (SHA)

Closer to the city center (30 minutes), mainly serving domestic and regional Asian routes.

💡 Tip: Book flights 4-8 weeks in advance for the best fares. Anhe Meige can help advise on convenient arrival times to coordinate with your appointment schedule.

🚗 Airport Pickup Service

Anhe Meige provides complimentary airport pickup for international patients:

  • Private car with professional driver waiting at the arrival gate
  • English-speaking assistant to help with luggage and navigation
  • Direct transfer to your hotel or the clinic
  • Available at both Pudong (PVG) and Hongqiao (SHA) airports
  • Return airport transfer also available on departure day

To arrange pickup, simply provide your flight details at least 48 hours before arrival via WeChat or WhatsApp.

🗣️ Medical Interpreter Service

Clear communication is essential for your cosmetic surgery journey. Anhe Meige provides:

  • English-speaking interpreter available during all consultations and procedures
  • Pre-consultation briefing to ensure your questions and concerns are fully addressed
  • Post-procedure translation for care instructions and follow-up guidance
  • 24/7 WhatsApp/WeChat support for any urgent communication needs

Our interpreters are experienced in medical and cosmetic surgery terminology, ensuring nothing is lost in translation.

Shanghai is a major cosmetic surgery hub with several well-known clinics including Shanghai Namei Plastic Surgery Hospital, Shanghai Qimei Plastic Surgery Hospital, and others. However, when traveling abroad for cosmetic procedures, language support and logistics are critical — Anhe Meige is one of the few clinics offering comprehensive English and Vietnamese interpretation, visa assistance, and full-service patient care throughout your journey.

📅 Step-by-Step Process

4-8 Weeks Before

Online Consultation

Contact Anhe Meige via WeChat or WhatsApp for an initial consultation. Share your goals, photos, and medical history. Receive a personalized treatment plan and cost estimate.

3-4 Weeks Before

Visa & Travel Booking

Apply for your China visa. Book flights and accommodation. Anhe Meige assists with visa support documents and hotel recommendations.

Arrival Day

Airport Pickup & Check-in

Private car pickup at the airport. Settle into your hotel. Rest and prepare for your consultation the following day.

Day 1-2

In-Person Consultation

Meet your surgeon at the clinic for a detailed consultation. Discuss your goals, review the treatment plan, and finalize details. Pre-procedure medical check-up.

Day 2-3

Procedure Day

Undergo your cosmetic procedure with our experienced medical team. Post-procedure monitoring at the clinic.

Day 3-7

Recovery in Shanghai

Rest and recover at your hotel. Follow-up visits to the clinic for check-ups and dressing changes. Enjoy Shanghai's amenities as you recover.

Departure Day

Final Check-up & Departure

Final consultation with your surgeon. Receive detailed aftercare instructions. Airport transfer to your departure flight.

Post-Return

Remote Follow-up

Scheduled video call check-ups to monitor your recovery. Ongoing support via WeChat/WhatsApp for any questions or concerns.
