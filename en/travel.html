✈️ Flights to Shanghai

Whether you are planning Vietnam to Shanghai plastic surgery or traveling from anywhere else, Shanghai is served by two major international airports with direct flights from cities worldwide:

Pudong International Airport (PVG)

Shanghai's primary international airport, serving most long-haul flights. Approximately 60 minutes by car from the city center.

  • From Asia: Tokyo (3h), Seoul (2h), Singapore (5h), Bangkok (4h), Manila (3h)
  • From Europe: London (12h), Frankfurt (11h), Paris (12h), Amsterdam (11h)
  • From Americas: Los Angeles (13h), New York (15h), Vancouver (12h), Sydney (10h)

Hongqiao International Airport (SHA)

Closer to the city center (30 minutes), mainly serving domestic and regional Asian routes.

💡 Tip: Book flights 4-8 weeks in advance for the best fares. Anhe Meige can help advise on convenient arrival times to coordinate with your appointment schedule.

🚗 Airport Pickup Service

Anhe Meige provides complimentary airport pickup for international patients:

  • English-speaking assistant to help with luggage and navigation
  • Direct transfer to your hotel or the clinic
  • Available at both Pudong (PVG) and Hongqiao (SHA) airports
  • Return airport transfer also available on departure day

To arrange pickup, simply provide your flight details at least 48 hours before arrival via WeChat or WhatsApp.

Clear communication is essential for your cosmetic surgery journey. Anhe Meige provides:

Our interpreters are experienced in medical and cosmetic surgery terminology, ensuring nothing is lost in translation. This is part of our commitment to safe plastic surgery in Shanghai for foreigners — clear communication is essential for patient safety and satisfaction.
